Three-level answer:
1. Perception fails, measurably.
 Frontier VLMs/LLMs reach 20–61% character accuracy where HERA reaches 80% — and they fail by 
auto-correcting the child's errors away
.
2. The cause is structural.
 The language prior is baked into decoding — strict "do not correct" prompting does not switch it off. mlphon likewise rejects 43.5% of learner spellings.
3. So LLMs take their proper role.
 Reasoning over verified evidence, planning, content, explanation — on top of a deterministic evidence chain they cannot replace.
Design principle: 
error preservation must be architectural, not prompted.
Agents orchestrate deterministic tools (HERA, OSUR-G2P, statistics) — they do not perceive or compute trends in-context.

=== SLIDE 6 ===
Problem Statement
Orthographic and phonological language difficulties are the most prevalent yet least screened form of SLD among Indian primary-school children — and they surface in everyday handwriting, where the letters may be well-formed but what is written is linguistically wrong. Handwriting is the sensor, not the disorder. Existing computational approaches (a) target English/Latin adult handwriting, (b) auto-correct away the very errors that carry the diagnostic signal, and (c) stop at one-time detection — a binary label — with no explanation of which difficulties the child shows, and no tracking of whether help is working.
This research proposes an identification–insight–monitoring framework: error-preserving recognition (HERA) and error-preserving G2P (OSUR) feed an agentic diagnostic layer holding a longitudinal learner model; each new sample is compared against the child's own history to produce actionable insights and progress verdicts; an agentic intervention layer delivers personalized, tiered remediation measured by the same pipeline — a closed loop under teacher supervision, transferable to other akshara scripts.
🔍
Which difficulties?
Specific orthographic vs phonological error patterns, classified against a diagnostic schema
🎯
What to work on?
Teacher-actionable, evidence-traced insights — priorities, not labels
📈
Is it working?
Improving / stagnant / worsening — each child vs their own history, not just population norms

=== SLIDE 8 ===
COMPLETED · UNDER REVIEW
P1 — HERA: Error-Preserving Recognition
Design:
 retrieval-only recognition — the language-model prior is removed from the loop entirely.
• Hierarchical 3-tier segmentation (VPP → Canny/contours → width fallback)
• Hybrid descriptor: frozen SigLIP-2 (768-d) + HOG (1,764-d) → 2,532-d vector
• HNSW nearest-neighbour retrieval + inverse-distance-weighted voting
• No lexicon, no beam search, no decoder → 
the retrieved label is the output
Knowledge base:
 336,527 images · 634 Malayalam grapheme classes (public on Hugging Face)
0.17–0.20
CER on unseen children's handwriting (200-word / 50-word sets)
+14.1 pp
accuracy gain of hybrid descriptor over SigLIP-2 alone (ablation)
Outperforms 
Gemini 2.5 Pro, Sarvam AI, GPT-5.1, Claude Opus 4
, and two PaddleOCR variants — by 18.8 to 60 CER points
Why it matters for the thesis:
 each output character is traceable to a retrieved neighbourhood — not a hidden sequence prior. Substitution, omission, and vowel-sign errors are preserved verbatim for downstream analysis.

=== SLIDE 10 ===
PROPOSED · DOCTORAL CORE
P3 — The Agentic Diagnostic-Intervention Layer
Longitudinal Learner Memory
 — structured per-child, per-error-class history built from HERA+G2P snapshots. Agents reason over this memory; they never compute trends in-context.
Error-Analysis Agent
Calls HERA + OSUR-G2P as tools; retrieves similar linguist-labeled cases (~1,000-instance gold set); classifies each error against the diagnostic schema with evidence traces.
Insight Agent (self-verifying)
Queries memory; calls deterministic stats tools (slope, change-point, mastery); issues improving / stagnant / worsening verdicts; critique–revise loop checks each claim against raw data.
Intervention Planner + Content Agent
RTI/MTSS-tiered plan (Tier 1→3); generates Malayalam practice AND the next probe, verified by OSUR/mlphon so drills target the intended phoneme contrast.
Orchestrator + Teacher Gate — every plan and insight passes human review; screening and support, never diagnosis
Probe mode:
 10–15 grade-leveled dictation/picture-naming words every 2–3 weeks (mirrors RTI progress monitoring). The Content Agent generates the next probe — the intended word is never missing inside the loop. Free-writing analysis is scoped as future work.
